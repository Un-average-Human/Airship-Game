# Airship Game

<br>

## How did I think of this?
Actually, I didn't! I was playing a game called <i>Delivery Not Guaranteed</i> on Roblox when I started thinking about ways of improving the gameplay. I landed on making it a co-op game instead with mechanics similar to <i>Dear Passengers</i>. Friends and janky physics always go well right?

## How to test it?
​WASD to move
LMB to interact
spin mouse in a circle to move valve (front most valve is gas and back most valve is vent (venting makes you lose your gas FOREVER)
A and D to steer airship once interacting with the helm
hold LMB to drag package

K to move airship and turn plane engine on
A and D to roll plane
W and S to pitch plane
Q and E to yaw
you wont get on the plane, i havent added that functionality yet so youll move with the inputs you use for the plane

MULTIPLAYER IS EXCLUSIVE TO STEAM! to play with others the host must click the host button and invite other players ALREADY IN THE GAME (in the menu screen, not hosting)
